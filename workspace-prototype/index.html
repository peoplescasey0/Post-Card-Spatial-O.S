POST CARD SPATIAL OS · OPERATOR HANDBOOK (V0.1)

User Manual & Field Guide

Reference guide for 3D card navigation, multi-OS hypervisor controls, Eco RAM thinning, and custom operator notes.

CH 01 3D Deck, Zoom & 6-Way Card Flip ▾
GRAB & DRAG CARDClick and drag left/right/up/down on any card to physically flip across all 6 faces.
CLICK / Z KEYZoom directly into the selected card (2× Retina OLED view). Press Z or Esc to zoom out.
SHIFT + DRAGReposition a card in 3D space above the studio floor.
CH 02 Multi-OS Hypervisor & Eco RAM Thinning ▾
MULTI-INSTANCERun up to 6 concurrent Windows 11, Linux, and Android cards. Secondary Windows cards auto-clone from your base disk with isolated TPM 2.0 state.
🍃 ECO RAM MODEStreamlined default allocation: 2 GiB for Windows, 1 GiB for Linux & Android (cycle via Playbooks & Ops ▾ → 🍃 Eco RAM).
VIRTIO BALLOONVMs include virtio-balloon for guest memory reclamation when supported by the guest. Host page merging is disabled for compatibility.
CH 03 Paper Airplanes, Heavy File Drops & Swarm Ops ▾
📦 HEAVY PLANESAttach real files (up to 512 KB) to mount inside the target VM's QEMU Virtual FAT (VVFAT) drive, or press Not now / Esc to cancel.
⏱️ SAVE / ⏪ REWINDCapture live RAM + disk checkpoints before risky commands and rewind in one click.
🐝 SWARM & PIPEFork 2× copy-on-write clone cards or execute commands over ttyS0 / COM1 with SHA-256 flight-recorder hashes.
CH 04 Custom Manual Placeholder & Operator Notes ▾

Saved automatically in local storage so you can expand your official User Manual anytime.
