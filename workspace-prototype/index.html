    POST CARD SPATIAL OS · OPERATOR HANDBOOK (V0.1)

    User Manual & Field Guide

    Reference guide for 3D card navigation, multi-OS hypervisor controls, Eco RAM thinning, and custom operator notes.

    CH 01 3D Deck, Zoom & 6-Way Card Flip ▾
    GRAB & DRAG CARDClick and drag left/right/up/down on any card to physically flip across all 6 faces.
    CLICK / Z KEYZoom directly into the selected card (2× Retina OLED view). Press Z or Esc to zoom out.
    SHIFT + DRAGReposition a card in 3D space above the studio floor.
    CH 02 Multi-OS Hypervisor & Eco RAM Thinning ▾
    MULTI-INSTANCERun up to 6 concurrent Windows 11, Linux, and Android cards. Secondary Windows cards auto-clone from your base disk with isolated TPM 2.0 state.
    🍃 ECO RAM MODEStreamlined default allocation: 2 GiB for Windows, 1 GiB for Linux & Android (cycle via Playbooks & Ops ▾ → 🍃 Eco RAM).
    VIRTIO BALLOONVMs include virtio-balloon for guest memory reclamation when supported by the guest. Host page merging is disabled for compatibility.
    CH 03 Paper Airplanes, Heavy File Drops & Swarm Ops ▾
    📦 HEAVY PLANESAttach real files (up to 512 KB) to mount inside the target VM's QEMU Virtual FAT (VVFAT) drive, or press Not now / Esc to cancel.
    ⏱️ SAVE / ⏪ REWINDCapture live RAM + disk checkpoints before risky commands and rewind in one click.
    🐝 SWARM & PIPEFork 2× copy-on-write clone cards or execute commands over ttyS0 / COM1 with SHA-256 flight-recorder hashes.
    CH 04 Pro AV Studio Rack, Stereo VU & Color Grading ▾
    🎛️ AV SUITEClick Room tools → Studio AV Suite to adjust master volume, sound cards, and lighting.
    🔊 STEREO VU METERSDual-channel (CH 1 L / CH 2 R) peak audio meters react in real-time to master volume and VM activity.
    🎨 4-WAY COLOR GRADEInstantly switch studio lighting: Twilight Atelier (deep slate & amber), Cyberpunk SecOps (electric violet & neon cyan), Matrix Terminal (phosphor green), or Vintage Rolodex (warm brass).
    📺 CRT SCANLINESToggle vintage phosphor scanlines and curved glass reflection overlay anytime.
    CH 05 DirectLink 10.0.0.2 & Native KVM Acceleration ▾
    ⚡ DIRECTLINKHypervisor delegates heavy workloads over the 1 Gbps direct link to Fedora 45 Workstation (10.0.0.2).
    🍏 NATIVE MACOS KVMmacOS boots via OpenCore UEFI + AppleSMC container with /dev/kvm acceleration, reaching desktop in ~15s.
    🔌 AUTO-TUNNELSWebSocket (5700) and QMP (4444) auto-tunnel over SSH with seamless local loopback fallback.
    CH 06 Front Bench Mode & In-Place 3D Twist ▾
    🪑 BENCH · 1/2/3 CARDSUse the topbar bench selector or SecOps Lab button to pull 1, 2, or 3 cards side-by-side to the front row.
    ⤢ TWIST EXPANSIONDrag any display corner or press T to twist and enlarge the guest viewport in 3D space.
    🛡️ KALI RED TEAMPulling SecOps Lab activates Kali Cyber Range on isolated multicast VLAN (230.0.0.1:4550).
    CH 07 3D Reversible Paper Bin & Zero-Data-Loss Trash ▾
    🗑️ 3D PAPER BINRemove any card by clicking Bin or dragging toward the corner trash can; the card folds into an airplane and flies into the bin.
    ↺ ZERO-DATA-LOSSBinned cards retain full QCOW2 disk state and can be restored back to the turntable anytime.
